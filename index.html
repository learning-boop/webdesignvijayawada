FAQ

Questions, answered

How much does a website cost in Vijayawada?

It depends on the number of pages, features and content you need. After a free 20-minute call we send a fixed quote in writing — no hidden costs, and no work starts until you approve it.

How long does it take to build a website?

A business website usually takes a few weeks; larger e-commerce or portal projects take longer. You get a dated timeline before we start, and we stick to it.

Will my website work well on mobile phones?

Yes. Most of your customers will visit on a phone, so we design mobile-first and test every page on real Android and iPhone devices before launch.

Do you help with Google ranking and local SEO?

Every site we build ships with technical SEO, fast load times and Google Business Profile guidance. Ongoing SEO and ads are available as monthly plans.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes. We build on editable platforms and walk you through updating text, photos and offers. We can also maintain it for you.
