Our mission

Great design shouldn’t be a luxury

Most digital agencies are built for big brands with big budgets. We built our studio for everyone else — the shop owner in Bengaluru, the clinic in Pune, the clothing label in Surat trying to reach customers online.

We believe great design and smart marketing should not be a luxury. Our mission is to close the gap between what Indian small businesses look like and what they deserve to look like — so they can compete, grow, and thrive.

Our story

From a design studio to a full digital partner

  1. 2008

    Founded in Vijayawada as a graphic design studio serving local businesses.

  2. 2012

    Expanded into digital — websites, social media, and early SEO for Indian SMEs.

  3. 2017

    Launched performance marketing practice. First international client (UAE).

  4. 2020

    Went fully remote. Doubled team size. Added WhatsApp automation and e-commerce builds.

  5. 2024

    Opened presence in the UK. Serving clients across India, UAE, and Europe.

  6. 2026

    2000+ projects. Building the definitive digital studio for Indian small business.
